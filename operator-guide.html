Social Media Auto-Posting — Kaam Karne Ka Tareeqa

Tum Google Sheet me post likhte ho — system khud sahi din aur waqt pe Facebook aur Instagram pe post kar deta hai. Neeche poora tareeqa hai.

Ek Baat Yaad Rakho

Tumhara sirf ek kaam hai: Sheet me post theek se bharna. Baaki (post karna, waqt dekhna) system khud karta hai. Bas har post ki row me Status me pending zaroor likhna — warna wo post nahi hogi.

Rozana

Sirf 3 Kaam

1

Image Google Drive me daalo

  1. Buongiorno Posts folder (Google Drive) me apni image upload karo.
  2. Image pe right-click → Share → Copy link.
  3. Yeh link Step 2 me Image_Link me daalna hai.

Folder pehle se "Anyone with the link" pe set hai, is liye us folder ki har image apne aap post ke qabil ho jati hai.

2

Sheet me nayi row bharo

Sheet ke Posts tab me ek khali row me har column bharo (neeche table dekho). Zyada tar columns me dropdown hai — likhne ke bajaye select karo.

3

Bas — ho gaya

System har 5 minute me Sheet check karta hai aur post ko uske waqt pe kar deta hai. Thodi der baad Status column dekh lo — hara (posted) matlab ho gaya.

Ehtiyat

Karo / Mat Karo

✓ Karo

  • Har post ki row me pending zaroor likho
  • Har platform ki alag row banao
  • Hashtags har post pe thoda badlo (bilkul same nahi)
  • Image ka Drive link "Anyone with the link" wala ho
  • Date double-click se chuno (calendar)

✕ Mat Karo

  • Ek hi caption teeno platform pe copy-paste (spam signal)
  • Cell ko ek column se doosre column me copy/drag (dropdown kharab ho jata hai)
  • Gray columns (Status ke baad wale) me kuch likhna
  • Roz ki hadd se bohot zyada posts ek din pe
  • Purani posts ko chhodna — Status dekhte raho
