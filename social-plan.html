Content Ka Format

Har post ka apna alag title aur text hota hai (image ke naam se banta hai) — tumhare maujooda posts ke bilkul usi style me:

  • Facebook — Buongiorno! ☀️ {tafseel}: una nuova immagine di {category}… + 👉 Scaricala gratis qui: {page link}. Page ka link caption me, aur image pe koi link nahi (photo post).
  • Instagram — Buongiorno! ☀️ {tafseel} — {category} 💛 + 🔗 Scarica gratis dal LINK IN BIO. IG pe links click nahi hote, is liye "link in bio" wala tareeqa.
  • Hashtags — har category ke apne (jaise #buonlunedì #lunedì), plus aam wale. Instagram pe 16 tak, Facebook pe 6–7.
  • Images — 240/240 unique, koi image dobara nahi.
